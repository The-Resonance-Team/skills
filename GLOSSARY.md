# Team rule language

The language for the team's coding guidance and its consumers.

## Language

**Consumer**:
A person or agent that uses the team's rules and workflows.

**Rule module**:
A group of team rules that applies to a shared audience or subject.

**Rule**:
A specific team standard that applies within a defined scope.

**Workflow**:
A procedure that guides a consumer through a task.

**Rule priority**:
The precedence of a rule or workflow when applicable guidance conflicts.

**Mandatory**:
The priority of guidance that takes precedence over recommended guidance.

**Recommended**:
The priority of guidance that applies unless it conflicts with mandatory guidance.

**Rule conflict**:
A contradiction between applicable rules that cannot both be followed.

**Trigger prefix**:
The opening phrase that selects a Claude Design deliverable type.
